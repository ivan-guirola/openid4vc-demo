            The token the cinema received
            
    
              One SD-JWT made of three parts joined by ~: the card signed by City Hall, the pieces you chose
              to reveal, and a proof signed by your wallet for this purchase.
            
    
            
    As it arrived
    
            
    
            
    1. The card, signed by City Hall
    
            
    The fields are hidden: only their fingerprints (_sd) travel here.
    
            
    
            
    2. What you revealed
    
            
    Each piece is a salt, a field name and its value. Its fingerprint must be in the card.
    
            
    
            
    3. The proof from your wallet
    
            
    Signed with the key the card was issued to, for this cinema and this purchase only.
